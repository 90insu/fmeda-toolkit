Read before quoting any figure above

  • PMHF is a lower bound. Single-point and residual terms only. The dual-point failure term needs explicit pair enumeration and is not modelled.
  • The conservatism level is a set of deterministic derating rules, not a statistical confidence interval. The same inputs at the same level always give the same numbers. It does not mean the result is 60, 90 or 99 percent likely.
  • Failure rates are flat at the profile temperature. IEC 61709 stress factors are not applied yet, so changing the temperature profile currently changes the report's provenance record and nothing else.
  • Targets come from ISO 26262-5:2018 Tables 4, 5 and 6, which are informative. Confirm them against your own copy before relying on them.
  • The bundled example is fictional and deliberately misses its ASIL D target at the nominal level. A first-pass architecture that does not close is a more useful demonstration than one that does.
